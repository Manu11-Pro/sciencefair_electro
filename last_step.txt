🧵 The Final Audio Jack Wiring Steps

1. Ground Connection: Solder a short wire from the GND Pin of your audio socket (the pin right next to the front metal barrel) straight into your board's main GND rail track (connected to Pin 4 of your chip) [tech-support].
2. Left Signal Connection: Take the wire coming from the Negative (-) side of your 47µF capacitor and solder it directly onto the Stereo Left O/P Pin on your socket [tech-support].
3. Leave Empty: Leave the broken Stereo Right O/P Pin and both Switched pins completely empty and floating [tech-support].
